## Who we are

OPEN Chicago brings together entrepreneurs, professionals, and changemakers through authentic connections, mentorship, and collaboration. We are based in Oak Brook, IL, and serve the Chicagoland and Midwest community.

## What we do

- **Annual Summit & Pitch Competition**: our flagship conference and startup pitch competition. [summit.open-chicago.org](https://summit.open-chicago.org/)
- **Events**: networking, workshops, and community gatherings. [Upcoming events](https://www.open-chicago.org/upcoming-events)
- **Mentorship**: experienced founders and leaders supporting the next generation.
